# Kids and minors

## Account holder age

The account holder must be 18+. People under 18 can watch only on a profile created by an adult account holder.

## Maturity ratings

Kids profiles filter by maturity rating. The account holder sets the highest rating allowed for each profile, and titles above it are hidden.

## Data from children

Lumen+ does not ask for personal information on kids profiles and does not use their viewing to personalize ads, because Lumen+ has no ads.
