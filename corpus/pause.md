# Pausing your membership

## How a pause works

You can pause up to 3 months instead of cancelling. While paused you are not charged and you cannot stream. Your profiles and watch history are kept.

## How often

You can pause once per 12 months. The 12 months count from the start of your last pause.

## Ending a pause

The membership resumes automatically at the end of the pause and you are charged on that day, which becomes your new billing date. You can also resume early in Account > Membership.

## Who can pause

Pausing is available on monthly plans only. Annual plans and memberships in a free trial cannot be paused.
