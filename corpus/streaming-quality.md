# Streaming quality

## Recommended internet speeds

Recommended speeds: 5 Mbps HD, 15 Mbps 4K. Slower connections still work; the video quality adjusts automatically.

## Quality by plan

The highest quality you can get depends on your plan: HD on Basic, Full HD on Standard, 4K on Premium. 4K also needs a 4K-capable screen and device.

## Saving data

On mobile apps you can choose Save data in App settings > Video quality. It uses about 0.3 GB per hour.
