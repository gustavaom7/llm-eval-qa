# Refunds

Lumen+ payments are generally not refundable. Refunds are given only in the cases below.

## First payment

You can get a refund only within 14 days of first payment, and only if under 2 hours watched in total on the account since that payment. This applies once per account. Request it through the in-app chat.

## Duplicate charges

If you were charged twice for the same period, duplicate charges are refunded in full, at any time, once confirmed by support.

## How long refunds take

Refunds take 5–10 business days to appear, depending on your bank or PayPal.

## What is not refunded

Unused days after cancelling, partial months, annual plans after the first 14 days, and gift card purchases are not refunded. Support agents cannot make exceptions to this policy.
