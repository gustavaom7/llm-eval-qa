# Supported devices

## Where Lumen+ works

Supported: iOS 16+, Android 10+, smart TVs, web browsers. Smart TVs include recent Samsung, LG, Sony and Android TV models. Web browsers include the current versions of Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge.

## Unsupported devices

Older devices may install the app but are not supported, and playback quality or features may not work. Lumen+ does not support jailbroken or rooted devices.

## Number of devices

There is no limit on how many devices you sign in to. The number of screens streaming at the same time depends on your plan.
