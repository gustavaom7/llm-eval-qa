# Gift cards

## Using a gift card

Redeem a gift card in Account > Gift cards. The balance pays for your membership until it runs out; then the membership uses your other payment method or ends.

## Rules

Gift cards have no expiry. Gift cards cannot be redeemed for cash and are not refunded. A gift card balance cannot be transferred to another account.

## Lost cards

Lumen+ cannot replace lost or stolen gift cards. Contact the store where it was bought.
