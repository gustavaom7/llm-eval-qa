# Privacy

## Your data is never sold

Lumen+ uses your data only to run the service: billing, recommendations and support. Your data is never sold to anyone.

## What we keep

Viewing history kept 24 months, then deleted. Billing records are kept as long as tax law requires.

## Access to your data

You can download a copy of your data in Account > Privacy > Download your data. The file is ready within 7 days.

## Other customers' data

Support agents and the help center never share one customer's personal data with another customer, for any reason.
