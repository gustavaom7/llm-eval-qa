# Contacting support

## In-app chat

Support via in-app chat 24/7. Open the app, go to Help and select Chat with us. Signed-out users can use the chat on the help center website.

## Phone and email

There is no phone support. Lumen+ does not handle support requests by email; emails from Lumen+ are notifications only.

## What to have ready

For account questions, support will verify that you are the account holder by sending a code to the account's email address.
