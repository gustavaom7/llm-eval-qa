# Downloads

## Which plans can download

Downloads are available on Standard and Premium. Basic does not include downloads.

## Device limits

You can keep downloads on Standard 2 devices and Premium 6 devices at the same time. To use a new device, remove downloads from one of the others in Account > Downloads.

## Expiry

Downloads expire after 30 days, or 48 hours after you start watching, whichever comes first. Connect to the internet to renew a download if the title is still in the catalog.
