# Cancellation

## How to cancel

You can cancel any time in Account > Membership. Select Cancel membership and confirm. There is no cancellation fee.

## Access after cancelling

You keep access until the end of the paid period. After that date you are not charged again and you can no longer stream.

## Refunds when cancelling

Cancelling does not trigger a refund. There are no partial refunds for the unused days of a monthly or annual period. See the Refunds page for the only cases where a refund applies.

## Coming back

You can restart your membership at any time. Your profiles, watch history and preferences are kept for 10 months after the membership ends, unless you delete your account.
