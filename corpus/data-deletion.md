# Deleting your account and data

## How to delete

You can delete account in Account > Privacy > Delete account. Deleting cancels your membership immediately and cannot be undone.

## What happens to your data

Your data erased within 30 days of the request. Billing records required by tax law are kept separately until that obligation ends.

## Before you delete

If you only want to stop paying, cancel or pause instead: deleting removes your profiles and watch history for good.
