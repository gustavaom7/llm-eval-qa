# Plans and prices

Lumen+ offers three plans. All plans include the full catalog, no ads and unlimited streaming. Prices are in US dollars and include taxes where required by law.

## Basic

Basic costs $7.99/month. You can watch on 1 screen at a time, in HD.

## Standard

Standard costs $12.99/month. You can watch on 2 screens at a time, in Full HD, and download titles on 2 devices.

## Premium

Premium costs $17.99/month. You can watch on 4 screens at a time, in 4K with HDR when available, and download titles on 6 devices. Family sharing is only available on Premium.

## Annual billing

Every plan can be billed annually instead of monthly. With annual billing you get 2 months free: you pay for 10 months and watch for 12. Annual plans renew automatically each year unless you cancel.
