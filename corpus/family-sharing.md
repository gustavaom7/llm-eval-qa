# Family sharing

## Who can use it

Family sharing is available on Premium only. Basic and Standard accounts cannot share with people outside the account holder's profiles.

## Limits

You can share with up to 4 profiles in one household. Everyone sharing must live at the same address as the account holder. Lumen+ may ask a device to confirm it belongs to the household.

## Managing members

The account holder adds and removes members in Account > Family sharing. Removed members lose access immediately; their profile and history are kept for 30 days in case they are added again.
