# Account security

## 2-step verification

Turn on 2-step verification in Account > Security. After you sign in with your password, Lumen+ asks for a code sent by email or text message.

## Signing out everywhere

If you think someone else is using your account, sign out of all devices in Account > Security, then change your password.

## What support will never ask

Lumen+ support will never ask for your password or your full card number. Support cannot share any information about an account with someone who is not the verified account holder.
