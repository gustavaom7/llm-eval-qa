# Profiles

## How many profiles

Every account can have up to 5 profiles per account. Each profile has its own watch history, recommendations and My List.

## Kids profiles

When you create a profile you can choose the kids profile option. Kids profiles only show titles rated for children and hide search results above the chosen maturity rating.

## Profile locks

Any profile can be protected with a 4-digit PIN in Account > Profiles. The account holder can reset a forgotten PIN after confirming the account password.
