# Changing your plan

You can change your plan in Account > Membership > Change plan.

## Upgrading

An upgrade takes effect immediately. The price difference for the rest of the current billing period is prorated and charged right away. From the next billing date you pay the full price of the new plan.

## Downgrading

A downgrade takes effect at the next billing date. Until then you keep the features of your current plan. No refund or credit is given for the remaining days of the higher plan.

## Annual plans

On an annual plan, an upgrade is prorated for the remaining months. A downgrade applies at the next annual renewal.
