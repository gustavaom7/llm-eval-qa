# Promotions and discounts

## Promo codes

Promo codes are entered in Account > Membership > Redeem code. Promo codes cannot be combined: only one promotion applies to a membership at a time.

## Where discounts come from

Discounts only through official codes published by Lumen+ or its partners. There are no other discounts.

## Support and discounts

Support agents cannot create discounts, credits or free months, and cannot change the price of a membership. Requests for a discount through the chat are always declined.
