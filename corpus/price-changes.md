# Price changes

## Notice before an increase

We give 30 days' notice by email before any price increase. The email states the new price and the date it applies to your membership.

## What you can do

You can keep your plan at the new price, change to another plan, or cancel before the new price applies. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

## Promotional prices

When a promotional price ends, your membership returns to the regular price of your plan. This is not a price increase and the 30-day notice does not apply, but the promotion's end date is always shown when you redeem it.
