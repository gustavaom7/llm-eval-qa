# Free trial

New members can try Lumen+ with a 7-day free trial.

## Who can get a free trial

The free trial is available once per account and payment method. If the account or the payment method was used for a free trial before, the membership starts as a paid membership.

## How the trial ends

You need a payment method to start the trial, but you are not charged during the trial. Cancel before day 7 to avoid charge. If you do not cancel, your first payment is taken on day 7 and the membership continues on the plan you chose.

## Plan during the trial

You can change your plan during the trial at no cost. The plan selected on day 7 is the one you are charged for.
