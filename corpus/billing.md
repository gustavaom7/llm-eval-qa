# Billing

## Billing date

You are billed on the signup day each month. If you signed up on the 31st and a month has fewer days, you are billed on the last day of that month. Your next billing date is shown in Account > Membership.

## Payment methods

Accepted: card, PayPal, gift cards. Gift card balance is used first; the rest of the charge goes to your card or PayPal.

## Failed payments

If a payment fails, the failed payment is retried 3 times over 7 days. You keep access while we retry. If all 3 retries fail, the membership is put on hold until you update your payment method in Account > Membership.

## Receipts

A receipt is emailed after every successful payment. You can also see your billing history in Account > Billing history.
